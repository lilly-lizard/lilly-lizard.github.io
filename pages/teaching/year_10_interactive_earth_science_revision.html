1. Stratovolcano

Composite Cone

Steep-sided volcano formed from alternating layers of thick lava flows, ash, and rocks. Often explosive.

Ex: Mount Ruapehu / Taranaki 🌋
2. Shield Volcano

Broad Shield

Broad, dome-shaped volcano built from fluid basalt lava flows. Gently sloped and quiet eruptions.

Ex: Rangitoto Island 🏝️
3. Caldera Volcano

Supervolcano Basin

A massive crater basin formed by the total collapse of a magma chamber roof during extreme eruptions.

Ex: Lake Taupō Basin 🌊
