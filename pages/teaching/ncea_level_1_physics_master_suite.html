Active Energy Forms

Involves immediate motion or movement of particles/waves
  • Kinetic Energy (Ek): Energy due to macroscopic motion of an object with mass (Ek = ½mv²).
  • Heat / Thermal Energy (Eth): Kinetic energy of vibrating microscopic particles (E = mcΔT).
  • Light / Electromagnetic: Energy carried by electromagnetic photons across space.
  • Sound Energy: Kinetic energy transmitted via pressure vibrations in a medium.

Potential (Stored) Energy Forms

Stored energy ready to be transformed into active forms
  • Gravitational Potential (Ep): Stored due to position in a gravitational field (Ep = mgh).
  • Elastic Potential: Stored when a spring or elastic material is stretched or compressed.
  • Chemical Potential: Stored in chemical bonds of food, batteries, and fuels.
  • Nuclear Energy: Stored in atomic nuclei released during fission or fusion.

Gravitational Potential Energy

Ep = mgh

Energy stored by position in a gravitational field. Higher initial height h provides greater total mechanical energy budget.

Kinetic Energy & Speed

Ek = ½mv²

Higher kinetic energy directly correlates to faster rolling or sliding speed (v ∝ √(Ek)).

Work Done & Heat Dissipation

Wfric = ffric × d = ΔEth

Friction force opposes motion along distance d, converting mechanical energy into microscopic thermal kinetic energy (heat).
