Rock Cycle Alchemist

Learn how heat, pressure, weathering, and cooling transform geological structures.

Identify the Three Key Rock Families

Rock Group A

Formed when hot molten magma or lava cools and hardens.

Rock Group B

Formed by layers of weathered fragments deposited, compacted, and cemented.

Rock Group C

Formed deep underground when rocks are subjected to high heat and extreme pressure.
